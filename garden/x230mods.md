# X230 Thinkpad Mods
One of the reasons I like my [X230 Thinkpad](/garden/mylaptop) is how much you can mod it. Here are all the modifications that I've done to mine in chronological order.

- When I bought it used, it already had 8GB RAM and a 256GB SATA3 SSD.
- I replaced the terrible TN panel with a slightly less terrible IPS panel. I used a panel with product number LP125WH2(SL)(B3).
- I replaced the 6 cell battery with a 9 cell battery I bought from [Duracell Direct](https://www.duracelldirect.com/). Pricy but gives me 5+ hours of battery life.
- I replaced the standard charging module with USB-C from [here](https://www.tindie.com/products/mikepdiy/lenovo-charging-port-type-c-pd-x220-x230/). I can easily charge my laptop with any 65W USB-C phone charger.
- My model was an X220 before I changed I changed it's motherboard with one from an X230. Instructions [here](http://panacek.net/post/frankenpad_x225/).
- After I "upgraded" to an X230, I followed [these steps](https://www.thinkwiki.org/wiki/Install_Classic_Keyboard_on_xx30_Series_ThinkPads) to support my old X220 keyboard.
- I 3D printed a Express Card Dummy card to fit in the Express Card slot, to reduce chassis flex in that area a little.
- I found an Genuine NMB US X220 Keyboard on ebay in good condition for a great price. Essentially the best keyboard ever made for that model.
- I replaced the Bluetooth 3.0 chip with a Bluetooth 4.0 chip I bought on AliExpress.
- I changed the default Trackpoint with a Soft Rim Trackpoint I bought on ebay. Huge Difference.

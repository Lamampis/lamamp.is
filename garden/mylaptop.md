# My X230 Thinkpad

I use a Thinkpad X230 from 2012 as a portable daily driver. It looks outdated as fuck and it probably is. But I wouldn't take any other laptop over it.

**Why?**

It does (almost) everything right. The keyboard, form factor, trackpoint, I/O, repairability, durability, [modability](/garden/x230mods). If I could [design a perfect laptop](/garden/perfectlaptop), it would be more similar to this over any other laptop out there right now.

For now, it still has all the computing power I need for my everyday tasks, even light gaming such as Minecraft. I will be using it for the forseeable future, up until something better gets released (unlikely), or it breaks (even more unlikely).

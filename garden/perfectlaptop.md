# What would a Perfect Laptop look like?

Let's say that I get the opportunity to create a perfect laptop for my needs. What would that laptop look like?

- 12-13 Inch, 144hz+ Refresh Rate, 3:2 display.
- Minimum 8 Hours of Battery Life.
- 7 Row Backlit Keyboard with PBT Keycaps, good travel.
- Soft Rim Trackpoint with dedicated trackpoint buttons.
- Replaceable RAM and Storage.
- Removeable Battery.
- Easily Repairable, everything held together with screws.
- Durable, IBM Thinkpad level durable
- USB C, HDMI, Ethernet, Audio Jack.
- ARM Processor (if they get better support from Linux OS's).

For now, no laptop model out there even gets close. I'll stick to using my [old Thinkpad](/garden/mylaptop).

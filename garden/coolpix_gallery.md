I recently purchased a used Nikon Coolpix L28 digital camera. It's a budget digital camera from 2013 that takes really cute photos. It uses AA batteries which I prefer over proprietary batteries and chargers, and it's very simple to use. Here are some of my favourite pictures I've taken with it so far.

I really enjoy carrying this little camera around, despite never having an interest in photography. Pictures taken with it are warm and vibrant, and have a soft, familiar quality that feels more personal as opposed to smartphone camera pictures. These older cameras have CCD sensors, which I won't act like I know what they are, but they create a unique aesthetic that is associated with Y2K nostalgia. I'm certainly really happy with it.

Thank you to the creator of [classicdigicam.com](https://classicdigicam.com), whose website had a lot of useful information that helped me pick a digital camera.

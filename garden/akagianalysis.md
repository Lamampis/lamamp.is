# Akagi Shigeru Analysis: The True Outlaw
Akagi Shigeru from the anime/manga Akagi, has quickly become a contender for my favourite character in media.
He is unique, mysterious and impossible to truly understand. For those in the story who meet him, he changes the way they think
forever. For as puzzling as he is, he feels real. They way he lives his life is unthinkable to everyone. In this blogpost, I'll attempt to understand this character.

*This includes **Major** spoilers for the Akagi anime/manga, as well as the Ten manga. Please enjoy these works before reading this.*

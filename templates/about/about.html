About website:

I made this website because I believe that everybody should have their own personal corner on the internet. The early internet was full of personal webpages that were so fun and full of unfiltered creativity, which is something that has mostly faded in modern time in favor of centralized social media profiles. In recent time, there has been somewhat of a revival of personal webpages and I was inspired by countless other websites to do the same.

I started developing this website on August 2024 for a bit, and picked it back up on June 2025. It's official birthday is on August 3rd 2025 when it first went live.

Javascript is used for the cursor trail and analytics using Umami, you can safely disable it. I only store cookies for theme preference. This website is open source, you can view the source code on my github.
